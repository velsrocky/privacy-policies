Vaulto — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-06 · Application: com.velstech.vaulto
TL;DR: Vaulto has no network permission. It cannot send your data anywhere. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDK. Everything lives encrypted on your device, and deleting the app deletes everything.

1. What we do NOT collect

Vaulto requests zero network permissions — the OS prevents it from making any network connection. We do not collect, transmit, or process:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Your vault contents, master password, or any secret
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics IDs
  • Location (precise or approximate)
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, or any "phoning home"
  • Biometric images (fingerprint / face data never leave the sensor)

There is no Vaulto server that receives user data, because the app never uses a network.

2. What is stored, and where

All data is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage:

DataLocationProtection
Your secretsApp-private file vaulto.encAES-256-GCM; key from Argon2id over your master password
Master passwordNever storedUsed in memory to derive the key, then discarded
Biometric unlock materialAndroid Keystore / iOS KeychainRandom wrapping key, gated by your device biometric
PreferencesApp-private preferencesNon-sensitive

The key and decrypted secrets live in RAM only while unlocked and are cleared on lock. (Dart gives no hard zeroization guarantee; see §7.)

3. Biometrics

If you enable biometric unlock, Vaulto uses your device's built-in fingerprint/face system (Android BiometricPrompt / iOS LocalAuthentication). Vaulto never sees, stores, or transmits biometric data — matching happens inside secure hardware. Vaulto only receives a success/failure result.

4. Clipboard

Copying a password places it on your system clipboard. Vaulto automatically clears the clipboard 30 seconds after each copy to limit exposure.

5. Backups and exports

  • Cloud/device backup is disabled. Android auto-backup and adb backup are off; the iOS vault file is excluded from iCloud Backup.
  • Manual export is your choice. You may export an encrypted .vaulto backup or a plaintext CSV; protecting exported files is your responsibility.

6. Pro license (offline)

Vaulto Pro is unlocked with a one-time license key verified entirely offline against a public key bundled in the app. We operate no licensing server. The key encodes a salted hash of the purchase email for support; your device never transmits it.

7. Security limitations (honest disclosure)

Vaulto uses standard, vetted primitives (Argon2id, AES-256-GCM, Ed25519) and has had an internal security review, but has not undergone an independent third-party audit. On a rooted/jailbroken or malware-compromised device, local encryption reduces to the strength of your master password. Choose a strong, unique master password. If you lose it, your data is unrecoverable — by design.

8. Children's privacy

Vaulto collects no personal data from anyone, including children. It requires no account and is not directed at children.

9. Data deletion

Uninstalling Vaulto removes all app data. The in-app Wipe vault action irreversibly destroys the vault and key material immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

Changes update the effective date and ship with an app update. Because Vaulto has no network access, changes reach you only through an update.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the address on the Vaulto store listing.
